# On-site checklist — one page, print it

Fifteen minutes the night before, thirty minutes at their place. Tick as you go.

## The night before

- [ ] `https://<console>/login` works
- [ ] `https://<api>/api/health` returns 200
- [ ] Their account created at `/signup` (their name, their website, an email and a
      password you chose)
- [ ] Their origin approved: Ajustes → *Tu web* → `https://their-site.es` (and `www` if it
      serves too)
- [ ] Sticky note in your pocket: their phone number, their opening hours, the price of
      their most common service
- [ ] Printed: the tag, the calendar feed link, one page of what it does and costs

## At their place

1. **Do not start with the product.** Ask what happens when someone calls while they are
   busy. Write down the number they say.
2. **Show it on their own site.** Your laptop, their URL, widget open. Ask it a real
   question out loud, then book it, then say the reference aloud.
3. **Say the limits before they ask:**
   - it does not answer the phone,
   - it does not take payment,
   - bookings live in your diary plus a read-only calendar feed, **not** inside their
     practice software,
   - it never gives medical advice.
4. **Fill the settings with them:** services, prices, hours, how they like to talk.
   → dashboard → Ajustes.
5. **Install the tag** before `</body>` of their site — or email it to whoever owns the site
   and get ten minutes on a screen share.
6. **Calendar:**
   - [ ] IN: their private iCal link pasted in Connections
   - [ ] OUT: the feed subscribed in *their* Google Calendar, on their laptop, before you leave
7. **Verify in an incognito window, on their domain:** bubble appears, question answered,
   booking lands in the dashboard. *No bubble means not installed.*

## Before you walk out

- [ ] They have the dashboard login and have opened it once themselves
- [ ] They have seen a booking appear in their own calendar
- [ ] They have your number and know what happens if they cancel
- [ ] You have said, in words, that the assistant does not answer the phone

## That evening

- [ ] Their dashboard: at least one conversation, at least one booking
- [ ] Write down what the assistant got wrong — that list is the roadmap
